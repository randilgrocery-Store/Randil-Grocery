1. WHAT YOU GOT  (the two computers)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Your system uses TWO machines working together over your shop
Wi-Fi:

  * THE ADMIN LAPTOP  - "the owner's computer"
      - Keeps ALL the records (the main database)
      - Full access: sales, stock, reports, suppliers, refunds,
        expenses, settings
      - Powerful dashboard with charts to see how the shop is doing
      - Bluetooth / thermal receipt printer

  * THE CASHIER POS  - "the billing counter computer"
      - Large touch screen for fast billing
      - The cashier logs in with their own name
      - Sees the sales counters for the day and can do GRN
      - Customer Display shown on the second screen (see below)

They connect automatically. Switch on both machines, open the
program on both, and they find each other by themselves.

3. WHAT THE SYSTEM CAN DO
-----------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE LISTING: watch out for big notes - the things marked  ***
are the special, "super cool" features.

*** CUSTOMER DISPLAY (second screen) ***
While the cashier is billing, the SECOND MONITOR in front of the
customer automatically shows:
   - Your shop logo and "Randil Grocery" branding
   - The items being scanned, one by one
   - Subtotal, discount and TOTAL AMOUNT in big letters
So customers can watch their bill as they shop. The cashier can
switch it on or off anytime from the dashboard.

*** PROFESSIONAL DASHBOARD (admin laptop) ***
The moment you open the system you see:
   - A personal greeting ("Good Morning...") with today's date
   - TODAY'S REVENUE and today's sale count in one glance
   - Six big colour cards: Revenue, Transactions, Estimated
     Profit, Margin, Units Sold, Stock at Risk.
       Revenue card even shows if you are up or down vs the
       previous period (+12.5% style) - see trends without
       doing any maths
   - Revenue chart for Today / 7 days / 30 days (one tap)
   - Top selling items, Inventory health pie chart
   - Low stock alerts, Cashier leaderboard (who sold most)

*** BILLING (POS) like a supermarket ***
   - Type or scan the barcode - item appears instantly
   - Touch screen friendly, works fast on a stand
   - Cash OR Card OR split payment (part cash + part card)
   - Automatically tells you the change to give
   - Prints a proper receipt (shop name, address, phone, thank-you
     message) on your thermal printer
   - Stock is deducted from the OLDEST items first (first-in
     first-out) so older stock sells before it expires

*** STOCK & INVENTORY ***
   - Products with barcode, buying price, selling price
   - Stock arrival is recorded with GRN (Goods Received Note) -
     the cashier can receive new stock too
   - Low stock and "out of stock" warnings on the dashboard
   - Batch management: track batches, expiry, and stock that was
     received against Purchase Orders

*** PURCHASES & SUPPLIERS ***
   - Create Purchase Orders to your suppliers
   - Keep a list of all suppliers with contact details
   - When goods arrive, receive them into stock quickly

*** REFUNDS & RETURNS ***
   - Customers return items? Process a refund properly
   - Refunds need approval (owner) to prevent misuse
   - Returned stock is put back on the shelf automatically

*** CUSTOMERS, EXPENSES, REPORTS ***
   - Keep a simple customer list (especially for credit customers)
   - Record daily shop expenses (electricity, staff, etc.)
   - Reports: daily and monthly totals, sales by cashier, best
     selling items, payment method breakdown (cash vs card) -
     printable for your records

*** BACKUP & SAFETY ***
   - Your data is saved on the admin laptop
   - One-click backup to a memory stick / folder
   - Soon: automatic password-protected backup to the internet so
     even if the laptop dies, nothing is lost

*** BILLING SCREEN IMPROVEMENT ***
   - The payment buttons (TOTAL AMOUNT, Paid / Change, PAY, HOLD,
     CLEAR) are now fixed at the bottom of the billing screen - no
     matter what, they never scroll out of reach when the billing
     list is long.

*** MOBILE APP (the "Randil Grocery POS" app on YOUR phone) ***
   - Free to install, NO login to remember - you open it and it
     shows the shop.
   - You can switch the look between Light / Dark and pick a colour.
   - See LIVE data from the shop even while you are at home:
       Home      - today's takings, charts, best selling items
       Sales     - every bill made at the shop
       Stock     - all products + which are running LOW
       Reports   - refunds, stock arrivals, expenses, customers
       Settings  - change theme / colours, refresh, reconnect

4. QUICK "HOW DO I..."
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Open billing ............. Login as admin or cashier > tap POS
  See today's takings ...... Admin laptop > Dashboard (top right)
  Add a new item ........... Admin > Inventory > Add Product
  Receive new stock ........ Cashier or Admin > GRN > + New
  Refund an item ........... Admin or Cashier > Refunds
  Print yesterday's report . Admin > Reports > pick date > Print
  Close the customer screen . Cashier dashboard > "Close Customer
                             Display" button
  Connect the two PCs ...... Settings > Multi-PC & Live Sync
                             (Turn ON server on the laptop, find
                             the server on the counter computer)
  Turn ON phone sharing .... On the laptop: Settings > Cloud
                             > enable + Save & Sync Now
                             (one-time, set up by your technician)
  Open the phone app ........ Tap the "Randil Grocery POS" app
                             > first time: enter your shop details
                             (one-time, set up by your technician)

      + your shop name. Done!
